Principles

Automate the chores, keep the craft

Frames, quality, calibration and stacking happen on their own. Finishing is yours.

Smart defaults, nothing locked

Defaults come from the object type, filter and the data itself. Every one can be overridden.

Two speeds of change

Finishing edits take seconds. Stacking settings like drizzle are rare, and queue a rebuild.

Quick and deep are one thing

Quick sliders and the Workshop edit the same recipe, so the two never drift apart.

Human-made pictures

AI helps build and test astrolab. It doesn't edit your picture: diagnostics yes, "fix it for me" no.

Data tells capture what to do

Processing knows when a target needs more time, and says so where you plan the night.
